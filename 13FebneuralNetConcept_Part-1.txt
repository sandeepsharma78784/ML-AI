Question: jese regression me derivative nikalte he ese hi classifiction me bhi derivative nikalete he kya
Answer:  ✅ Short Answer:
            Haan. Bilkul nikalte hain.
            Training ke time har neural network me derivative lagta hi lagta hai — 
            chahe regression ho ya classification.
        Training ka goal hota hai: loss km krna 
            loss km krne k liye model ko adjust kna pdega.
            model ko adjust krne k liye har weight ko kuch bdna ya ghatana pdega.
            and pdana he ya ghatana he ye uske derivative k sign se pta chlta he
            derivative + means weight bdane pr losss bdega
            derivative - means weight bdane pr losss ghatega.
            🔹 1️⃣ Regression Case
                    Loss = Mean Squared Error (MSE) 
                        L=(y−y^​)2

                        iska derivative ya to positive ya negetive aayega.
                        and weight ki info y ki definition me rahehi.
            🔹 2️⃣ Classification Case
                    Binary Classification:
                            Loss = Binary Cross Entropy:
                                L=−[ylog(y^​)+(1−y)log(1−y^​)]
                            iska derivative ya to positive ya negetive aayega.
                            and weight ki info y ki definition me rahehi.
                    Multi-class Classification:
                            Loss = Categorical Cross Entropy
                                Softmax + Cross entropy ka derivative bhi nikalta hai.
                                Interesting fact 🔥
                                Softmax + CrossEntropy ka derivative simplify ho jata hai:
                                    dZdL​=y^​−y
                        Exactly regression jaisa form 😄
        🔥 Important Insight
                    Training ke time har jagah ye hota hai:
                    Forward pass
                    Loss calculate
                    Backpropagation
                    Derivatives calculate
                    Weights update
                    Chahe regression ho
                    Chahe classification ho
                    Chahe LLM ho
                Sab me derivative lagta hai.
            💡 Strong Line Yaad Rakho
                    Agar derivative nahi niklega, to learning nahi hogi.
            Derivative loss function ka.

Question: humara focus weight ko bdana he ya ghtana he
Answer: Hamāra focus weight badhāna ya ghatānā nahi hai.
        Hamāra focus LOSS kam karna hai.
        Weight to sirf ek knob (control) hai jise ghuma kar loss kam karte hain.
        means ghata bhi skte he and bda bhi skte he [jis point pr bethe he us pr depend krta he]
            means us point/example pr derivative ki value pr depend krega.

        🧠 To weight kab badhega, kab ghatega?
            Ye depend karta hai derivative ke sign par.

            derivative positive:
                    weight bdane se loss bdega
                    to hum iska ulta krege kyuki humko loss km krna he.
            derivative negetive:
                    weight bdane se loss ghatega
                    to hum esa hi krege kyuki humko loss km krna he.
