Reason 1: why transpose input matrix to calculate gradient of W from lowercase_delta and input.
so ab hum dekhte he ki model ya phir weight kese update krna he:
    iske phle ye read karo : 'how dL/dw calculated? Loss in neural network.'
    is se ye pta chla jayega ki dL/dw ko matrix form me kese kre.
    to dL/dw= lowercase_delta*x aata he sb calculation krne k baad 
    ab humare paas 
        lowercase_delta he in form of n*1 matrix.
        x he in form of m*1
    is se model nikalna he: vo model jiska multiply x se krne se lowercase_delta bna and 
        model*x=lowercase_delta;  // where lowercase_delta= y-y.
        model*(m*1)=(n*1)

        but we have to find model:
        model= (n*1)/(m*1) = lowercase_delta/x  // means x ka transpose krna pdega.
        model= lowercase_delta * x^T 
             = nx1 * 1xm = nxm bnega.
    
    to ek to ye reason he ki transpose kyu kiya.

How to calculate lowercase_delta in back_propagetion: for inner neurons
    Output layer ka delta BCE + Sigmoid me directly:y-y^ nikal jaata he.
    Lekin hidden layers ke neurons ka delta directly nahi pata hota,
    kyunki unke paas target value (y) nahi hoti. Isliye unka delta next layer ke delta se nikala jata hai.
    suppose a network:
           Input      Hidden1      Hidden2      Output
            400   --->   100   --->   200   --->   10
        Forward Pass:
                Layer 1
                    Input:
                        X = 400 × 1
                    Weight:
                        W1 = 100 × 400
                    Output:
                        Z1 = 100 × 1
                        A1 = 100 × 1
                Layer 2
                    Weight:
                        W2 = 200 × 100
                    Output:
                        Z2 = 200 × 1
                        A2 = 200 × 1
                Output Layer
                    Weight:
                        W3 = 10 × 200
                    Output:
                        Z3 = 10 × 1
                        A3 = ŷ = 10 × 1
        Backward Pass
            Step 1: Output Delta
                    Target bhi
                        Y = 10 × 1
                    Prediction
                        Ŷ = 10 × 1
                    To
                        δ3 = Ŷ − Y
                        Size = 10 × 1
                    Ye clear hai
            Step 2: Hidden2 (200 neurons):
                    Ab confusion yahi hota hai.
                    Tumhare 200 neurons me se ek neuron maan lo:
                    H157- har output me iska contribution he.
                    Ye sirf ek output se connected nahi hai.
                    Ye 10 output neurons se connected hai.
                              O1
                            /
                    H157 --- O2
                            /
                        O3
                        ...
                        \
                            O10
                    Output me 10 errors aaye.
                    H157 bolega:
                        "In 10 errors me mera contribution kitna tha?"
                        e1,e1,e3..........e10
                    Wo kya karega?
                        200*1 tha usne model  10*200  k sath milke 10*1 ka output bnaya tha.
                         200 neuron ka 200 model weight k sath multiplies hokr ek scalr value bni ouptput 1,
                          similarly o2,o3,.......o10.
                    to abhi humko us model and ye output jo he 10 ka iska use kr k lowercase_delta nikalna he.
                    to layer2 ka lowercase_delta:
                        tmp = transpose(model3) * lowercase_delta3;
                        lowercase_delta2 = tmp ⊙ sigmoid_dash(z2); // yaha 
                            //sigmoid_dash(z2) se element-wise multiply bhi karna padta hai.
                    sigmoid dash kyu?:
                            z  ---->  sigmoid  ---->  a  ---->  Loss
                                        σ(z)  
                        Forward Pass: sigmoid(z) ✅
                        Backward Pass: sigmoid_dash(z) ✅  // % constribution of that neuron in the ouptput error.
