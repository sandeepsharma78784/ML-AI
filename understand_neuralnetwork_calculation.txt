How to calculate lowercase_delta in back_propagetion: for inner neurons
    Output layer ka delta BCE + Sigmoid me directly:y-y^ nikal jaata he.
    Lekin hidden layers ke neurons ka delta directly nahi pata hota,
    kyunki unke paas target value (y) nahi hoti. Isliye unka delta next layer ke delta se nikala jata hai.
    suppose a network:
           Input      Hidden1      Hidden2      Output
            400   --->   100   --->   200   --->   10
        Forward Pass:
                Layer 1
                    Input:
                        X = 400 × 1
                    Weight:
                        W1 = 100 × 400
                    Output:
                        Z1 = 100 × 1
                        A1 = 100 × 1
                Layer 2
                    Weight:
                        W2 = 200 × 100
                    Output:
                        Z2 = 200 × 1
                        A2 = 200 × 1
                Output Layer
                    Weight:
                        W3 = 10 × 200
                    Output:
                        Z3 = 10 × 1
                        A3 = ŷ = 10 × 1
        Backward Pass
            Step 1: Output Delta
                    Target bhi
                        Y = 10 × 1
                    Prediction
                        Ŷ = 10 × 1
                    To
                        δ3 = Ŷ − Y
                        Size = 10 × 1
                    Ye clear hai

Question : final gradient kese nikal raha he and finally model update krne ka formula kya he.
Answer   : isko smjhne k liye phle regularize means kya and regularization_parameter kya?
            Regularize ka matlab hota hai model[weights] ko control karna taaki wo bahut zyada complex na 
            ho aur overfitting na kare.
            Regularize = model ki complexity ko limit karna / weights ko control karna taaki
            overfitting kam ho.
            but humko pta he ki weight me first column bias hota he 
            and activation layer me first neuron bias hota he.
            regularization_parameter me humko model se kaam he to model k bias ko dekhte he
            Weights → regularize hote hain ✅
            Bias → regularize nahi hota ❌
            isliye 
                bias wale column ko update krne ka alag formula and weights wale column ko update krne ka
                alag formula.
                final_gradient= learning_rate* ((1/m)*upper_case_delta + (reg/m)*theta)   // here theta means original model ya weights.
                to pure therta k sath regularization parameter ka multiplly nahi hoga.
                only theta k view k sath reg param ka multiply hoga isiliye 
            for(idx=0;idx<upper_case_deltas.size();++idx)

Question: upppercase delta and weights ka kya relation
Answer: Uppercase Delta (Δ) batata hai ki weights (models) ko kitna aur kis 
        direction me badalna hai.
        Forward propagation me ye weights prediction banate hain.
        Backpropagation me hume pata chalta hai ki prediction galat tha.
        Ab sawal hai:
            Kaunsa weight kitna change karna chahiye?
        Iska jawab Uppercase Delta deta hai.
        Step 1: Lowercase delta
                Output se error aati hai.
                δ = neuron error
                Ye batata hai neuron kitna galat tha.
        Step 2: Gradient
                Har weight ke liye gradient nikalte hain.
                    Gradient=δ×a^T
                menas output layer ka jo neuron he usko bnane me 
                uske pichle activation layer and model me galti hogi 
                to jo gati humne lower_case_delta me capture kri usko pichli activation layer k transpose 
                se multiply kr de, to ek naya model bn k aa jayega isi model ko tmp_upper_case_deltas 
                means ek training example se aane wali galti ko sudharne k liye change.
                ese sbhi training example k tmp_upper_case_deltas ko upper_case_delta me accumulate krte gaye
                finally upper_case_delta ko regula
                code me:
                    tmp_upper_case_deltas =   lower_case_delta * layerTranspose;
