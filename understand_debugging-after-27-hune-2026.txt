                    Matlab l3 directly l2 aur m2 par depend karta hai.

            Question ye hai:
                    Agar l3 me error aa gayi hai, to us error ke liye l2 kitna responsible hai?
                    Kyunki l3, l2 se hi bana tha, isliye hume error ko peeche (backward) l2 
                    tak bhejna padega.
                    Ye kaam m2 ke transpose se hota hai.
                ld1 = ld2 × transpose(m2)
            Iska matlab:
                ld2 batata hai output layer me error kitni hai.
                transpose(m2) batata hai ki output layer ka har neuron hidden layer ke kis neuron 
                    se kitna influence hua tha.
                In dono ko multiply karke hume pata chal jata hai ki l2 ke har neuron ne output 
                    error me kitna contribution diya.
                Agar activation function (jaise sigmoid) use ho rahi hai, to uska derivative bhi 
                    include karna padta hai:
                ld1 = (ld2 × transpose(m2)) ⊙ activationDerivative(l2)
                    Yahan ⊙ ka matlab element-wise multiplication hai.  

            Isi process ko Backpropagation kehte hain—error ko output layer se hidden layers 
            tak reverse direction me bhejna.
        similarly:
            ld0 = ld1 × transpose(m1)
